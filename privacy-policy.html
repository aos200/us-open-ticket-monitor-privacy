Privacy Policy

US Open Ticket Monitor

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Information We Use

The US Open Ticket Monitor uses the mobile phone number provided by the user solely to deliver SMS alerts about ticket opportunities detected by the user's ticket-monitoring application.

How Information Is Used

The phone number is used to send automated ticket-alert messages requested by the user. Ticket-marketplace information may be processed by the application to identify qualifying ticket opportunities. We do not sell personal information.

SMS Messages

SMS messages are sent through Twilio. Message and data rates may apply according to the user's mobile carrier plan. The user can stop receiving alerts by disabling SMS alerts or removing their phone number from the application.

Information Sharing

The user's phone number is not shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Twilio may process the phone number and message information as necessary to provide SMS delivery services.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information used by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be posted at this URL.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the ticket-monitoring application, contact the application owner through the GitHub repository associated with this project.
