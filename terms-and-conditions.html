US Open Ticket Monitor

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Program Description

US Open Ticket Monitor is a personal ticket-monitoring program that checks supported ticket marketplaces for qualifying US Open ticket opportunities and sends automated SMS alerts to users who have requested them.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on the user's monitoring settings and the availability of qualifying ticket opportunities. When multiple qualifying opportunities are detected during a monitoring cycle, they may be combined into a single SMS alert. Users should expect automated alerts only when qualifying opportunities are detected.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. The user is responsible for any SMS or data charges imposed by their mobile carrier or wireless service plan.

Opt Out

To stop receiving SMS alerts, reply STOP to any message or disable SMS alerts in the application. After sending STOP, the user will no longer receive automated SMS alerts unless they later re-enable the service.

Help and Support

For help with US Open Ticket Monitor SMS alerts, reply HELP to an SMS message or visit the project's GitHub repository: https://github.com/aos200/us-open-ticket-monitor.git .

SMS alerts are informational only and do not guarantee that a ticket remains available or that the displayed price remains unchanged. Any purchase is made through the applicable ticket marketplace and is subject to that marketplace's terms and conditions.
